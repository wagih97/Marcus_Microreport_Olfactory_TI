# Marcus_Microreport_Olfactory_TI

This repository accompanies the microreport describing the establishment of a head-fixed olfactory transitive inference task in mice. The interactive viewer presents the behavioral schedule, performance of the 12 mice, and individual lick responses during novel-pair testing.

Mice were trained on four overlapping premise pairs: AB, BC, CD and DE. They were then tested on the untrained AE and BD pairs without sucrose or quinine delivery. The viewer allows readers to follow performance across training and testing and examine the responses underlying the test results.

## Open the viewer

Download `marcus_microreport.html` and open it in a web browser. The file contains everything needed to run the viewer, including its fonts, and works offline after downloading. No installation is required.

## Explore the data

- **Behavioral schedule:** Follow the task over days 1–22, from premise-pair training through reinforcement, randomization, novel-pair testing and reassessment. Select **Enlarge schedule** for a closer view. The A > B > C > D > E diagram at the top of the page shows the trained premise pairs as solid arcs and the untrained test pairs, AE and BD, as dashed arcs.
- **Performance:** Hover over or tap the chart to see the mean ± SEM for that session together with every mouse’s value, sorted from highest to lowest. The odor pair of the session under the pointer is highlighted in the diagram at the top. After selecting the chart, the left and right arrow keys move between sessions. Select a mouse below the chart to highlight its curve. Black squares and error bars show the mean ± SEM across mice; the dashed horizontal line marks 50%.
- **Lick rasters:** Choose a mouse, test day and odor pair in the control bar near the top of the page; on larger screens it stays in view while you scroll. Test days 1 and 2 correspond to behavioral days 19 and 20. Each AE or BD panel shows the two odors separately and lists that mouse’s A- or B-directed licking and choice indices for the day. Black marks indicate individual licks, and red marks identify the first response-window lick on trials containing a response.
- **Lick detail:** Click a raster to inspect individual licks. Scroll to zoom in time (up to 256×), drag to pan, show a single trial, and hover over a lick to see its trial and time. **Counts and indices** lists the licks, responding trials and indices for both odors.
- **Reference raster and zoom:** In the lick detail, select **Reference raster** to open the original raster figure. Scroll over the area you want to inspect, drag to pan, or use the overview in the lower-right corner to move to another part of the plot. Zoom extends to 50× the fitted view while preserving sharp vector lines. Select **Fit** to return to the complete panel.
- **Downloads:** The performance chart, schedule, rasters and the current lick-detail view can be downloaded as SVG files using the controls in the viewer.
- **Light and dark themes:** Use the button in the top-right corner to switch themes. Charts and rasters stay on a white background in both.

Selecting an individual mouse highlights its performance curve without changing the group mean or SEM. Click “Mean ± SEM” to show or hide the mean line, markers, and error bars together. During randomization, performance is displayed in a common premise-pair order across mice; hover labels identify these pair-aligned positions rather than a shared experimental day.

## Methods and source data

The viewer provides an interactive way to inspect the behavioral data accompanying the microreport. Experimental procedures, performance and preference-index definitions, and statistical analyses are described in the manuscript and its accompanying source data.
