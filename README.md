# Marcus_Microreport_Olfactory_TI

This repository accompanies the microreport describing the establishment of a head-fixed olfactory transitive inference task in mice. The interactive viewer presents the behavioral schedule, performance of the 12 mice and individual lick responses during novel-pair testing, together with the design behind them: odor assignments, randomization sequences, and sucrose and quinine deliveries.

Mice were trained on four overlapping premise pairs: AB, BC, CD and DE. They were then tested on the untrained AE and BD pairs without sucrose or quinine delivery. Odor identities were counterbalanced across four groups of three mice, and each mouse followed one of four randomization sequences before testing. The viewer allows readers to follow performance across training and testing and examine the responses underlying the test results.

## Open the viewer

Download `marcus_microreport.html` and open it in a web browser. The file contains everything needed to run the viewer, including its fonts, and works offline after downloading. No installation is required.

## Explore the data

Links at the top of the page jump to each section.

- **Behavioral schedule:** Follow the task over days 1–22, from premise-pair training through reinforcement, randomization, novel-pair testing and reassessment. Select **Enlarge schedule** for a closer view. The A > B > C > D > E diagram at the top of the page shows the trained premise pairs as solid arcs and the untrained test pairs, AE and BD, as dashed arcs.
- **Performance:** Hover over or tap the chart to see the mean ± SEM for that session together with every mouse’s value, sorted from highest to lowest. The odor pair of the session under the pointer is highlighted in the diagram at the top. After selecting the chart, the left and right arrow keys move between sessions. Select a mouse below the chart to highlight its curve. Black squares and error bars show the mean ± SEM across mice; the dashed horizontal line marks 50%.
- **Lick rasters:** Choose a mouse, test day and odor pair in the control bar near the top of the page; on larger screens it stays in view while you scroll. Test days 1 and 2 correspond to behavioral days 19 and 20. Each AE or BD panel shows the two odors separately and lists that mouse’s A- or B-directed licking and response-allocation indices for the day. Black marks indicate individual licks, and red marks identify the first response-window lick on trials containing a response.
- **Lick detail:** Click a raster to inspect individual licks. Scroll to zoom in time (up to 256×), drag to pan, show a single trial, and hover over a lick to see its trial and time. **Counts and indices** lists the licks, responding trials and indices for both odors.
- **Reference raster and zoom:** In the lick detail, select **Reference raster** to open the original raster figure. Scroll over the area you want to inspect, drag to pan, or use the overview in the lower-right corner to move to another part of the plot. Zoom extends to 50× the fitted view while preserving sharp vector lines. Select **Fit** to return to the complete panel.
- **Odor assignments:** The grid lists the chemical, concentration and solvent at each position, A–E, for each of the four counterbalancing groups (Table S1). Groups 2 and 4 exchange the chemicals at B and D, marked B⇄D. Hover over or select a chemical to find it in every group. **Same chemical, different position** shows, for each exchanged chemical, the share of B-or-D responding that went to it when it held position B and when it held position D, as group means with individual mice. Use **Response-allocation** and **Licking** at the top of the section to switch between the two indices.
- **Randomization sequences:** The odor group × randomization sequence matrix places each mouse where its odor group meets its four-day sequence (Table S2) and shows its B-directed index pooled over both test days; the margins give group and sequence means ± SEM. **Every block, every mouse** shows performance in every block for every mouse over days 1–22, shaded from 50% or below (lightest) to 100% (darkest). Labels mark the pair blocks of days 15–18, the only days whose order differed between mice. Use **Rows** to order the mice by mouse, sequence or odor group; **Days 15–18** to switch between the order each mouse ran the blocks (**As run**) and the common pair order of the performance chart (**As plotted above**); and **Highlight** to pick out one premise pair or the AE and BD test blocks. Hover over a cell to see its value and the mean of all mice for that block.
- **Sucrose and quinine deliveries:** **Deliveries by odor** shows sucrose deliveries above the baseline and quinine deliveries below it for each odor, as the mean ± SEM with each mouse as a dot. Choose **Training**, **Reinforcement**, **Randomization** or **All pre-test** (days 1–18). Brackets give the Tukey-adjusted comparison of B and D. The tiles above the chart show the selected mouse’s total sucrose and quinine volumes and its B-directed response-allocation index. **Outcome history and preference** plots each mouse’s cumulative sucrose deliveries for B (**B sucrose**) or quinine deliveries for D (**D quinine**) against its B- or D-directed response-allocation index, with a least-squares line and the Pearson correlation.
- **Data tables:** Each chart in the odor, randomization and delivery sections has a **Data table** beneath it listing the values shown.
- **Downloads:** The performance chart, schedule, rasters, block calendar, delivery chart and the current lick-detail view can be downloaded as SVG files using the controls in the viewer.
- **Light and dark themes:** Use the button in the top-right corner to switch themes. Charts and rasters stay on a white background in both.

Selecting an individual mouse highlights its performance curve without changing the group mean or SEM. The mouse chosen in the control bar is followed through every section, and selecting a mouse in the matrix, the block calendar or the outcome scatter selects it throughout the page. Click “Mean ± SEM” to show or hide the mean line, markers, and error bars together. During randomization, performance is displayed in a common premise-pair order across mice; hover labels identify these pair-aligned positions rather than a shared experimental day. The block calendar shows the day on which each mouse ran each block. Charts in the odor, randomization and delivery sections can also be read from the keyboard: select a chart and use the arrow keys; in the block calendar and the outcome scatter, Enter selects the mouse at the cursor.

## Methods and source data

The viewer provides an interactive way to inspect the behavioral data accompanying the microreport. Experimental procedures, performance and preference-index definitions, and statistical analyses are described in the published paper. The source data are available as Additional file 2 (.xlsx) of the manuscript.
